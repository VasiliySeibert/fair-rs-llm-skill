## Outcome

Describe the actual result and the most useful next action. Do not infer overall
FAIR compliance from a file count or badge.

| Practice / narrow claim | Status | Evidence | Limitation | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Describe the claim | verified / failed / not_checked / not_applicable | File, command and result, revision, or record URL | Scope and uncertainty | Concrete next step |

## Changes and verification

List changed files and why they changed. Record commands and results; distinguish
schema checks, cross-file consistency, license coverage, and scientific execution.
Record expected outputs and tolerances separately from observed outputs.

## Publication and preservation

Record release tag/commit, deposit URL, version and concept DOI roles, archived
source revision/SWHID, and the status of each request. If not performed, say so.
Identify external inputs or environments not included in the archive.
